2D Pixel City Pack
by laavis
https://opengameart.org/content/2d-pixel-city-pack
License: CC-BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Used in Pomodoro City for the buildings (cut into ground floor, repeatable
floor and cornice), trees, bus, bus stop sign, rope barrier, city
silhouettes and the dusk sky. The sprites are converted to C arrays in
src/assets.c by tools/gen_assets.py; the windows of each floor are
recolored by the hour each session was finished. ICON0.PNG and PIC1.PNG are
compositions made from these sprites.
